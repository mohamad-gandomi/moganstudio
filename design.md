# Mogan Studio design notes

Mogan Studio is a focused, single-page Next.js studio site. The visual direction is editorial, calm, and practical: warm canvas backgrounds, dark ink sections, Manrope for interface text, and Newsreader italic for emphasis.

## Page structure

The page flows from Hero to Selected Work, Services, Contact, and the existing final call to action. The contact section has two prominent paths—WhatsApp for quick messages and Calendly for a scheduled 30-minute call—with email as a small fallback.

## Content principles

- Lead with WordPress, WooCommerce, performance, and custom web development.
- Keep project and service content local and easy to edit.
- Avoid unnecessary forms, dashboards, data collection, and third-party UI until they are needed.
- Keep the primary action clear on mobile and desktop.
